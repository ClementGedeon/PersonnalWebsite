Curriculum vitae

Clement Gedeon, PhD

Materials chemist · Postdoctoral Research Associate, King Abdullah University of Science and Technology

Experience

  • 2026 – now
    Postdoctoral Research Associate, KAUST Thuwal, Saudi Arabia

    New chiral conjugated materials for optoelectronics, with Prof. M. Heeney. Secured a US$250k grant for a UV-Vis-NIR CD/CPL spectrometer and develop bespoke sample stages and analytical techniques for chiral materials.

  • 2025
    Postdoctoral Research Associate, LCPO Bordeaux, France

    Metal-free synthesis of conjugated polymers, with Dr A. Gapin and Prof. C. Brochon. Designed a 3D-printed flow synthesis system and reached up to tenfold higher molecular weights than the previous state of the art. Supervised students and restored over €50k of instrumentation.

  • 2024
    General Consultant, Engage Brisbane, Australia

    Site inspection, troubleshooting and technical reporting for companies in the chemical, energy and mining sectors.

  • 2020 – 2023
    Research Engineer, ADVENT Technologies Patras, Greece

    Fuel cell materials and semiconducting polymers for photovoltaics. Instrument steward for 15+ instruments, laboratory management, safety officer, and first experience of industrial scale-up.

Education

  • 2020 – 2024
    PhD in Molecular Chemistry and Materials Science, University of Rennes

    Industrial doctorate in the Marie Skłodowska-Curie HEL4CHIROLED network, supervised by Dr J. Crassous and Dr C. L. Chochos. Awarded with the congratulations of the jury.

  • 2018 – 2020
    MSc in Molecular Chemistry, University of Rennes

    Highest honours. Valedictorian of the University of Rennes and ENSCR.
