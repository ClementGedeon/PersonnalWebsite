CEMSupra Workshop 2026 · RIKEN, Japan · November

The use of Chiral Conjugated Polymers in Material Science

Oral presentation and poster · Clement Gedeon, KAUST

About the talk

How chirality built into conjugated polymers and non-fullerene acceptors can be put to work in materials science: from the design of helicene-based monomers, to circularly polarised light emission, to the first organic solar cells whose performance improves because of donor-polymer chirality.
